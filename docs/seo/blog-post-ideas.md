# Blog Post Ideas: 100 Topics

As of 30 Sep 2026 · Companion to [seo-content-plan.md](seo-content-plan.md)

These are 100 ideas for the Premium Design Company blog, grouped by the 4 topic pillars in the SEO plan, plus a fifth group about working with a studio. Posts marked ★ are already scheduled in the plan's 6-month calendar. The rest are the backlog. By reader: 67 are for buyers, 19 for both buyers and peers, and 14 for peers.

## How to use this list

1. **Pick** the next idea. Start with buyer posts in the Cost and Vs formats, because they are closest to a sale.
2. **Check demand.** Type the main phrase into Google and note the autocomplete suggestions and the "People also ask" questions. Check the volume in Google Keyword Planner. If nobody searches for it, reword it or skip it.
3. **Check the top 5 results.** Only write it if we can do better, with real examples, numbers or a template they lack.
4. **Move it** into the calendar table in the SEO plan and follow the post structure there.
5. **Rewrite the working title** before publishing. These titles are starting points, not final headlines.

**Columns:**
- **Reader:** *Buyer* means founders and business owners who might hire us. *Peer* means designers and developers, who bring backlinks. *Both* means both.
- **Format:** matches the formats table in the SEO plan.
- **Case study** and **Data** posts need real client work or numbers from our own projects. Don't publish them with made-up examples.
